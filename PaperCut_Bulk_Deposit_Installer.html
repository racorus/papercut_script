PaperCut Bulk Deposit

  1. Log in to PaperCut Web Cashier: http://10.52.5.20:9191 and open Deposit. Payment method must be Cash.
  2. Click the PaperCut Bulk Deposit bookmark. A green panel opens on the right.
  3. Choose your list file (or paste from Excel) and click Check list. It shows the row count and total.
  4. Optional: Check users only looks up every username without depositing.
  5. Click Start deposits and confirm. Keep the tab open until it says Finished.
  6. At the end, the summary lists done / skipped / check rows. Click Download results (CSV) to keep a record.
Safety: each row is submitted once only and never retried. Unknown users or errors are skipped and the run continues. A row marked CHECK means no receipt appeared, so look in Order History before redoing it. If the tab closes mid-run, click the bookmark again and press Resume.
